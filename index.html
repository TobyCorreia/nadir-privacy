Nadir Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Nadir app for iOS

In short: Nadir reads your sleep and step data from Apple Health, with your permission, and uses it on your iPhone to estimate your circadian rhythm. Nothing leaves your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third parties.

What Nadir does

Nadir estimates the low point of your daily body-temperature rhythm (your "nadir") by running a published mathematical model of the human circadian clock on your own phone. It uses that estimate to show your rhythm on a 24-hour ring, to track how it drifts from night to night, and to suggest when to seek or avoid bright light if you have set a wake-time goal.

Data Nadir reads

With your explicit permission, granted through the standard iOS Health permission sheet, Nadir reads two categories from Apple Health (HealthKit):

  • Sleep analysis: when you were in bed and asleep, as recorded by your watch, ring or other sleep tracker.
  • Step count: steps recorded by your iPhone or wearable.

Nadir reads the most recent 14 days. It does not read any other Health category and it never writes to Apple Health.

Where the data goes

Nowhere. All processing happens on your device. Nadir has no server, makes no network requests, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party code. Your Health data is never transmitted, sold, shared, or used for advertising, marketing or any purpose other than showing you your own results.

What is stored on your device

  • Your wake-time goal and app settings, such as whether reminders are on.
  • The time of the last background refresh, used for diagnostics inside the app.

Nadir does not keep copies of your Health data. Each time it runs, it reads from Apple Health again and recomputes the result. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, Nadir schedules local notifications on your device for the start of each light window. These are created and delivered entirely by iOS on your phone; no notification service or server is involved.

Your control

  • You can withdraw Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps & Services → Nadir, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Nadir. Nadir will then show no estimate.
  • You can turn reminders off in Nadir's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Nadir.
  • You can remove all data Nadir stored by deleting the app.

Beta versions through TestFlight

If you use a pre-release version of Nadir through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage information and may pass crash logs and any feedback you choose to send on to the developer. That collection is governed by Apple's TestFlight terms, not by Nadir, and still does not include your Health data.

Children

Nadir is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Nadir is a wellness tool for curiosity and self-experimentation. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and its estimates should not replace advice from a doctor or sleep specialist.

Changes to this policy

If Nadir ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Because the app has no server, any such change would arrive only with a new version of the app that you choose to install.
